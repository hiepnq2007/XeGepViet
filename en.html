A cleaner experience

From booking request to completed ride

Xe Ghep Viet is designed around a simple flow: passengers create a request, the system finds suitable routes, drivers confirm, and both sides track the trip until completion.

  • Route and capacity based trip matching.
  • Support for shared seats, private rides, and parcels.
  • Trip history for follow-up and driver income statistics.
